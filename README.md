### What I do

- **Product & engineering leadership** — built the product, engineering, and design organization from scratch, and authored the company's AI roadmap: governance, training, pilot projects, internal tooling, and the AI products in the app
- **Marketplace platforms** — scaled a two-sided marketplace from 2,500 to 50,000+ credentialed professionals and from 3,000 to 20,000+ hours per week, from one state to 7+

- **Revenue & go-to-market** — as CRO before that, built the operational systems, market intelligence, customer-facing features, brand, and positioning the business runs on

### Public projects

Most of my current code lives in private repositories; the contribution graph shows the cadence. Two projects are public:

- **[signature-verification-cnn](https://github.com/k0rnacki/signature-verification-cnn)** — writer-independent signature verification on the CEDAR dataset: a signer catalogue, a Siamese CNN, a scan-artifact leak found and neutralized before training, and writer-level cross-validation instead of one lucky split
- **[call-toxicity-pipeline](https://github.com/k0rnacki/call-toxicity-pipeline)** — triage for customer-service call recordings: Whisper transcription, toxic-bert scoring, a threshold rule, run end to end on synthetic calls
